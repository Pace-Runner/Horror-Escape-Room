# Audio sources

Recordings kept for the team that the game does not load. They sit outside
`src/assets/`, so the voice checks (`npm run check:voices`) and the build
leave them alone.

- `mark-voice-takes/`: the 8 of Mark's ElevenLabs takes that don't play.
  Some lines are no longer played; some are said at the same moment by
  Daniel's recorded whispers; one take no longer matches its line's text.
  `docs/MARK_VOICE_LINES.md` gives the reason for each, and the steps to
  wire a take in. The other 21 play, from
  `src/assets/audio/Dont Let it out Mark voice/`.
